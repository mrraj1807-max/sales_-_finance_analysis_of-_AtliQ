🚀 Explosive Growth

AtliQ Hardware grew from ₹87.5M to ₹598.9M in just 2 years — a 204.5% YoY jump in FY2021, driven by strong demand across all regions and product categories.

⚠️ Margin Pressure

Gross Margin % declined from 41.4% (FY19) to 36.4% (FY21). Rapid scaling and competitive pricing in new markets are squeezing profitability.

🎯 Target Miss

Despite strong absolute growth, all 23 markets missed their FY2021 Net Sales targets. Poland (-18.1%) and Canada (-14.5%) saw the largest shortfalls.
